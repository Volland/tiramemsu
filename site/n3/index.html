About this book

Tiramemsu stores facts with their own ids, two clocks and a history that is never deleted. This book is about the next step: asking what follows from that memory, with N3 rules, and keeping what follows as memory too.

A plain rule engine joins facts that were never true at the same time, forgets which rules it used last month, and leaves its conclusions behind when their premises are retracted. The chapters show how tiramemsu avoids all three, one idea at a time, with examples you can paste.
